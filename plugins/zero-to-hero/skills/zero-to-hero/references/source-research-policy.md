# Source research policy

Research is required when product facts could be stale or high impact: laws, regulations, standards, competitor claims, hardware parts, manufacturing, security-sensitive integrations, medical/legal/financial claims, model/provider behavior, or new frameworks.

Research outputs must record:

- source URL or citation location;
- retrieved date when available;
- relevance;
- confidence;
- whether the source is canonical, supporting, or untrusted;
- decisions affected.

If web access is unavailable and research is required, stop and record the research queue instead of guessing.
